# Surface: aly.lat public site (home first, then inner pages)

Mode: Persuade, with the owner as the face of the brand (he makes content about himself; visitors must recognise him instantly). Audience: business owners, TJ and abroad, mostly on phones. Action: request form or WhatsApp. Proof: real cases and reviews only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A dark modern home at night, and he lives in it. Every section is a different room lit only by architectural light — LED lines, cove glow, a picture light, a lit island — and he is the person in the room. Refuses the white grid and the museum metaphor (rejected by the owner).
OWN-WORLD: Near-black matte surfaces (#070707 / #0d0d0d / #141414), warm 2700K line light (#ffd7a3 glow), rare cobalt cove light (#2d4dff), off-white text (#f3eee7). BBH Bogle headlines, Nunito Sans 300 corner notes, Pacifico in warm light colour. Glowing hairlines instead of borders; no cards.
STORY: Meet him, walk his rooms, see the work lit, switch on a direction, write.
FIRST VIEWPORT: His cut-out portrait at a black desk-island centre-right, under-glow strip below, ceiling LED rectangle drawing itself above; ALISHER GAFUROV huge behind him; request + WhatsApp bottom-left; corner note bottom-right. Visible at first paint.
FORM: Owner-pinned via four interior references (supersedes seed d1b6853e).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
