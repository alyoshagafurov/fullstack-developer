Mode: Persuade (portfolio that must convert). Audience: business owners, TJ and abroad, mostly on phones. Action: request form or WhatsApp. Proof: real published cases and testimonials only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A night museum of work. Each project hangs alone on a dark wall under its own spotlight; the visitor walks the halls instead of reading a landing page. Refuses the category default of a white grid of cards plus numbered process lists.
OWN-WORLD: Charcoal museum walls (#0F100F / #161816), bone text (#ECE7DD), warm spotlight pools, brass (#C8A46B) only for Pacifico curator notes and tiny markers. Bogle headlines huge, Nunito Sans 300 wall labels in corners, hairline frames, pill buttons in bone.
STORY: See the work lit, believe the craft, pick a hall (direction), leave a request or WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Giant two-line headline bottom-left, featured case lit top-right, wall label bottom-right, primary CTA and WhatsApp under the headline. Content visible at first paint.
FORM: Gallery white cube rendered dark (pick, position 1), seed d1b6853e.
